# TaskFlow — Privacy Policy

*Effective date: 31 October 2026*

TaskFlow ("the app") is a task-management app created by students of The Independent Institute of Education as part of a course project. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

## Information we collect
| Information | Why | Where it is stored |
|---|---|---|
| Name and e-mail address | To create and identify your account | MongoDB Atlas (cloud database) |
| Password | To let you log in. It is **hashed with bcrypt**; we never store or see the plain password | MongoDB Atlas (hash only) |
| Google account name and e-mail (only if you choose "Continue with Google") | To sign you in | MongoDB Atlas |
| Your lists, tasks, subtasks, due dates and settings | To provide the app and sync your data between devices | On your phone and in MongoDB Atlas |
| Streak and weekly completion counts | To show your progress | MongoDB Atlas |
| Device notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) | To send you notifications | MongoDB Atlas |

We do **not** collect your location, contacts, photos or advertising ID, we show **no ads**, and we do **not** sell or share your data for marketing.

## Services that process data for us
Google (Sign-In and Firebase Cloud Messaging), MongoDB Atlas (database hosting) and Render (API hosting). Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections.

## How long we keep it
Until you delete your account.

## Your choices and deleting your account
* Change your name, language and notification settings at any time in **Settings**.
* **Delete your account and all of its data:** open the app → **Settings → Delete account**. Your account, lists and tasks are permanently removed from our server and from your phone.
* If you can no longer open the app, e-mail us at **[your e-mail address]** from the address you registered with and we will delete the account.

## Children
TaskFlow is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.
